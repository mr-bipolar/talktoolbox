Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TalkToolbox handles information when you use the app.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

On this pageOverviewInformation and dataThird-party servicesText-to-speechNotificationsSecurity and retentionChildrenChangesContact

1. Overview

TalkToolbox is an English-learning app that provides vocabulary resources, an English–Malayalam dictionary, conversation examples, grammar lessons and text-to-speech functionality. The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

According to the information supplied by the developer, TalkToolbox does not ask users to provide an email address for account registration, does not request microphone access, and does not record users' voices.

2. Information and data

Although TalkToolbox does not provide account registration, the app uses third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting and push notifications. These services may collect or process technical and usage-related information from the app or device.

Depending on the service and its configuration, this may include:

  • Device and advertising identifiers or app-instance identifiers.
  • IP address and general network or device information.
  • App interactions, usage events, session information and advertising interactions.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic information, stack traces and relevant app or device state.
  • Notification subscription or delivery identifiers and notification interaction information.

TalkToolbox does not intentionally collect voice recordings through a microphone. Do not send sensitive personal information to the developer through app content or support channels.

3. Third-party services

TalkToolbox currently includes or uses the following services, as identified by the developer:

  • Google Mobile Ads: used to display advertisements. The advertising SDK may collect device identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, measurement and fraud prevention, depending on SDK version and configuration.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: used to understand app usage and improve the app. Analytics may process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and usage events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: used to diagnose crashes and stability issues. Crash reports may include stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata and an installation identifier.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: included in the app project. Data sent to or received from the database depends on the app's implementation and database rules. The developer should specify the exact content stored and accessed before publishing this policy.
  • OneSignal: used to provide push notifications. The service may process device or subscription identifiers, IP address, device information and notification delivery or interaction data.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the providers' policies for more information: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and OneSignal Privacy Policy.

4. Text-to-speech

TalkToolbox provides text-to-speech functionality. The app does not use this feature to record the user's voice. Speech output may be provided by a text-to-speech engine available on the device. The handling of text by that engine depends on the device, operating system and selected speech service; consult the relevant provider's privacy information for details.

5. Push notifications

TalkToolbox uses OneSignal to support push notifications. Your device may receive a notification subscription or delivery identifier so notifications can be sent. You can manage or disable notifications through your device's app notification settings.

6. Security, retention and deletion

Third-party providers describe the safeguards they use to protect information processed through their services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. The developer retains or accesses service data according to the app's configuration and the providers' retention controls.

Because TalkToolbox does not use account registration, there is no in-app account deletion process. To ask a question about information associated with the app or a notification subscription, contact the developer using the details below. Some information may need to be managed through the relevant service provider or device settings.

7. Children's privacy

The app's intended age group and whether it is directed to children must be confirmed by the developer. Advertising and analytics settings should be configured to comply with applicable child-safety and privacy requirements. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact the developer.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
